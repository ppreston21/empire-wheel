# Empire Wheel Vision

Empire Wheel is a structured learning and contribution platform
for history and the humanities.

Instead of passively consuming historical content, users progress
through a competency map by studying scholarship, examining primary
sources, making historical claims, and defending those claims with
evidence.

Long term, Empire Wheel should allow learners to develop enough
demonstrated competency to make meaningful contributions to historical
research and source analysis.

## Initial Product

The first live curriculum focuses exclusively on Ancient Mesopotamia.

The user should be able to:

1. View their historical competency wheel.
2. Enter the Mesopotamia curriculum.
3. Complete a lesson.
4. Examine a primary source.
5. Write an interpretation.
6. Receive structured AI feedback.
7. See progress reflected in the UI.

## Core Principle

Stop consuming history. Learn to do history.

Each module should teach learners how historians move from evidence to
interpretation: identifying what survives, making a defensible claim,
and distinguishing that claim from what the evidence directly shows.
