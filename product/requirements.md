# Empire Wheel v0.1 Requirements

## Home

Display an 18-spoke competency wheel. Only Mesopotamia is active; the
remaining spokes should appear locked or inactive.

Display overall progress, Mesopotamia progress, and modules completed.

## Mesopotamia Curriculum

Display 10 modules. For v0.1 only Module 1 needs complete content.

### Module 1 — From Village to City: Ubaid → Uruk

The page must contain, in order:

1. Learning objectives
2. Historical overview
3. Key concepts
4. Timeline
5. Primary-source/source-evidence section
6. Analysis exercise
7. Reviewer feedback
8. Completion/progress state

Curriculum content must remain separate from presentation components.

## Source-Evidence Exercise

Present a clearly labeled source-evidence summary when verified primary-source
content is unavailable. Do not invent quotations, translations, artifact IDs,
dates, or citations.

Ask the learner what evidence supports the argument that increasing economic
complexity required increasingly centralized administration. Provide a large
response field and require 300–600 words before submission.

## Mock Reviewer

After submission, show an explicitly mocked structured response containing:

- historical accuracy: 1–10
- use of evidence: 1–10
- reasoning: 1–10
- distinguishes inference: 1–10
- strongest observation
- unsupported claim
- missing evidence
- recommended next step

The reviewer must not provide an ideal answer before the learner attempts the
exercise.

## Progress

After submitting a valid analysis, mark Module 1 complete. Progress may be
stored locally in the browser and must appear in the module, curriculum, and
home interfaces.

## Out of Scope

Do not implement authentication, payments, social features, leaderboards,
peer or expert review, blockchain, a persistent database, a mobile application,
real AI calls, or additional complete modules.
