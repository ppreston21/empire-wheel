## Selection decision

The former Getty assignment was replaced because its recorded web-edition URL
returned HTTP 404. Both new assignments are bounded chapters in the openly
accessible institutional PDF for *Beyond the Ubaid*. The Institute for the
Study of Ancient Cultures publication page supplies the bibliographic landing
page, and the PDF supplies stable page-fragment links.

## Assigned readings

1. Robert A. Carter and Graham Philip, “Deconstructing the Ubaid,” pp. 1–10
   (PDF pp. 11–20), in *Beyond the Ubaid*, SAOC 63 (2010). Expected time: 25
   minutes. The assignment ends before the chapter's extended synthesis so the
   learner focuses on definitions, local variation, and problems with a simple
   core model.
2. Gil J. Stein, “Local Identities and Interaction Spheres: Modeling Regional
   Variation in the Ubaid Horizon,” pp. 23–35 (PDF pp. 33–45), ending before
   “Ubaid Ritual and Ideology.” Expected time: 25 minutes. This bounded section
   introduces the regional comparison and its warning against constructing a
   single composite society from evidence drawn from separate regions.
