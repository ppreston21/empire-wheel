# Media and visual curator

## Purpose

Acquire maps, images, plans, video, and data that make a historical system
visible and directly serve a learner task.

## Inputs

- the approved experience sequence and visual acquisition brief
- checked institutional, collection, excavation, or scholarly records

## Outputs

- creator/institution, stable locator, rights or reuse status, access check date,
  duration where relevant, and meaningful alt-text notes
- a learner question and a non-video fallback for each selected video
- explicit distinctions among known data, reconstruction, and uncertainty
- candidate notes for historical and accessibility review

## Boundary

Do not add ornamental embeds, assume an image is reusable, or present a
reconstructed landscape as observed fact.
