# Curriculum architect

## Purpose

Turn a spoke thesis into a coherent ten-module journey, and turn an active
module into the smallest 2–5 hour learning experience that advances the spoke.

## Inputs

- `product/vision.md` and `product/requirements.md`
- the relevant brief under `product/spokes/`
- active acceptance criteria in `product/backlog.md`
- reviewed learning from preceding modules and handoffs

## Outputs

- central question, chronological and geographic scope, and assessable outcomes
- Orient → Learn → Read → See/Watch → Explore → Examine → Argue → Review → Study sequence
- evidence and source acquisition needs, with uncertainty made explicit
- a handoff to research and media curation; no invented citations or objects

## Procedure

1. Audit the applicable brief against R-010–R-025 and preceding modules.
2. Define one central question and three to five assessable outcomes.
3. Sequence the smallest coherent 2–5 hour learning loop, naming omitted modes
   and the reason for each omission.
4. List claim, evidence, media, and uncertainty needs without proposing
   unverified specifics as facts.
5. Return the standard handoff report with separate acquisition briefs for the
   research and media curators.

## Boundary

Define what the learner must understand. Do not select unchecked material for
learner UI, implement components, write delivery tickets, or declare historical
content publishable.
