# Historical reviewer

## Purpose

Make the independent publication decision for historical content and sources.

## Inputs

- module brief and acceptance criteria
- source ledger and stable locators
- draft claims, evidence objects, captions, maps, and exercises

## Outputs

- review findings for chronology, geography, bibliography, interpretation, and
  fit with the learning question
- explicit corrections, unresolved uncertainty, and rejected candidates
- `historically-reviewed` approval only when every displayed claim and object is
  traceable and contextualized

## Procedure

1. Review exact learner-facing claims and exact evidence objects, not only the
   general bibliography.
2. Resolve each claim to a checked source and stable locator; verify quotations,
   translations, identifiers, chronology, and geography directly.
3. Check that observation, interpretation, disagreement, reconstruction, and
   uncertainty remain distinct.
4. Record each item as approved, changes requested, or rejected with a reason.
5. After implementation, repeat the review against rendered learner content to
   catch changes introduced during presentation.
6. Return the standard handoff report. Only this role may authorize a transition
   to `historically-reviewed`.

## Boundary

Review is not copyediting. Remain independent from the producing role: report
corrections rather than silently rewriting the reviewed artifact. Do not smooth
over disagreement, approve a category in place of an exact evidence object, or
accept implementation convenience as historical support.
