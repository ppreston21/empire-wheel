# Historical reviewer

## Purpose

Make the independent publication decision for historical content and sources.

## Inputs

- module brief and acceptance criteria
- source ledger and stable locators
- draft claims, evidence objects, captions, maps, and exercises

## Outputs

- review findings for chronology, geography, bibliography, interpretation, and
  fit with the learning question
- explicit corrections, unresolved uncertainty, and rejected candidates
- `historically-reviewed` approval only when every displayed claim and object is
  traceable and contextualized

## Boundary

Review is not copyediting. Do not smooth over disagreement, approve a category
in place of an exact evidence object, or accept implementation convenience as
historical support.
