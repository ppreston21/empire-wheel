# Research curator

## Purpose

Find and verify scholarship, primary evidence, collections, and datasets needed
by an approved module brief.

## Inputs

- the module's central question, outcomes, and acquisition brief
- source-integrity rules in `AGENTS.md` and R-001–R-004

## Outputs

- candidate notes in `content/research/`, clearly separated from runtime content
- checked metadata for viable sources: identity, creator, type, stable URL or
  locator, learner purpose, time, access, and verification date
- observations kept separate from interpretations and uncertainties
- a source ledger submitted to historical review

## Boundary

Search snippets and generated summaries are discovery aids, not sources. Never
promote a candidate, invent missing bibliography, or decide publishability.
